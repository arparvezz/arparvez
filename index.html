05. Questions, Answered

Should I build my website on WordPress or Shopify?

Choose Shopify if selling products is the core of your business and you want hosting, checkout and security handled for you. Choose WordPress (with WooCommerce if needed) when content, SEO flexibility, custom functionality or multilingual setups matter most. I build on both and will recommend the platform that fits your goals and budget.

Is SEO included when you build a website?

Every build ships SEO-ready: clean semantic HTML, a logical heading structure, schema markup, XML sitemap, optimized images, meta titles and descriptions, and strong Core Web Vitals. Ongoing SEO such as keyword research, content strategy and link building is available as a separate service.

How long does a WordPress or Shopify website take?

A landing page usually takes a few days, a business website one to three weeks, and a full eCommerce store two to five weeks depending on products, integrations and content. You get a clear timeline before work starts.

Can you make my slow website faster?

Yes. I audit Core Web Vitals, then fix the causes: heavy themes and plugins, unoptimized images, render-blocking CSS and JavaScript, missing caching and slow hosting configuration. Faster pages improve user experience, conversions and search rankings.

My WordPress site was hacked. Can you fix it?

Yes. I scan and remove malicious code, backdoors and spam injections, restore clean files, update core, themes and plugins, and harden the site with security measures to prevent re-infection. I can also help request removal of Google security warnings.

Do you work with clients outside your country?

Yes. I work remotely with founders, agencies and marketing teams worldwide, including clients in Europe such as Austria. Communication happens over email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn, and I respond within 24 hours.

Can you build a custom WordPress plugin for my business?

Yes, as an extra service. I build custom WordPress plugins using an AI-assisted (vibe coding) workflow combined with hand-written PHP and JavaScript, then reviewed and tested for security and performance. Typical plugins add custom tools, dashboards, calculators or integrations that off-the-shelf plugins can't handle.
